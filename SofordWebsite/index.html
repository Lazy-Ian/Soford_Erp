FAQ

Questions buyers ask us

What is your minimum order quantity?

Rubber mats start at 100–200 m², EVA mats at 200 pieces, and artificial grass at 1,000 m².

Can you put our logo and colours on the product?

Yes. We offer custom colours, EPDM fleck percentages, sizes and thicknesses, plus logo embossing or printing on tiles and private-label packaging.

Do you provide samples?

Yes. The sample fee is deducted from your first order.

How long does production take?

Production takes 7–20 days after the deposit is received. Trade terms are FOB Qingdao.

What certifications and warranty do you offer?

SGS and REACH. Artificial grass and rubber flooring have a 6–8 year warranty.

What payment terms do you accept?

30% T/T deposit.
