FAQ

Questions buyers ask us

What is your minimum order quantity?

Most rubber flooring starts at 100–200 m², EVA mats at 200 pieces and artificial grass at 1,000 m². Mixed products can be combined in one container — ask us.

Can you put our logo and colours on the product?

Yes. We offer custom colours, EPDM fleck percentages, sizes and thicknesses, plus logo embossing or printing on tiles and private-label packaging.

Do you provide samples?

Yes. Small samples are available for colour and quality checks; you only cover courier cost, which is credited against your first order.

How long does production and shipping take?

Production is usually 7–20 days after deposit. Sea freight time depends on your port; we can quote FOB Qingdao, CIF or door-to-door.

What certifications do your materials have?

Our materials are SGS tested and selected products meet EU REACH requirements. Test reports are available on request.

What payment terms do you accept?

T/T (30% deposit, balance before shipment), L/C at sight and Alibaba Trade Assurance.
